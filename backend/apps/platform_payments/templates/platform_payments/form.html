SaaS Payment Automation

When a payment is marked as paid, the system updates the selected company subscription, reactivates access if needed, and connects the SaaS payment with its generated document.

  • Only subscriptions from the selected company are shown.
  • Paid payments renew the selected subscription.
  • Expired company access becomes active again after a valid paid payment.
  • Pending payments do not activate access until they are marked as paid.
